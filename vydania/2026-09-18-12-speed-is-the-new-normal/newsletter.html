This issue - five things we shipped, and why fast is the new normal

Straight from the kitchen

We didn't spend the summer idle. Here's a pick of the things we managed to build.

The point isn't any single feature. It's that none of them were heroics or exceptions. This is just the speed we work at now - and that changes what we're even allowed to propose.
 

Speed stopped being the exception

When "a year" becomes "a few weeks," it moves the goalposts - for what we plan, decide, and dare to design.

So this is the list. Some of it came from a "we just need X" request and existed shortly after. Some are pieces of the bigger move off Siebel and other legacy systems - not unrelated toys, but a steady shift onto things we actually control.
01 Communication hub

It's deployed - and we're gradually moving every email and SMS into it. Today that traffic goes out from a pile of different places; the hub is the one box we stream it all through.

It's a strong lever for the CRM Transformation - a lot of that communication lives in Siebel today, but not only there.

Already live: the SMS to people interested in the new iPhone 18 have been going out - and keep going out - through the Communication hub.
02 A new IWD  (now STR)

IWD = intelligent workload distribution. The old one lived in Genesys and stopped working. Plenty of people told us replacing it was a year-long project.

It was built end to end over my vacation at the end of August. A month later it's deployed and in testing with business - so far, positive.

One more thing: the new IWD has a new name. From now on, it's STR - Smart Task Routing.
03 SearchX

For search on Digital we use a SaaS - Luigi's Box. We're planning to replace it with SearchX, a new in-house solution that does fast search. Right now I'm testing and comparing the two - if everything works the same for the customer, we'll make the switch.

But that's just the start. We're already discussing SearchX inside our voice agents like Olivia and WonderfulAI. It's one of the main pillars for agents that talk to customers - the data in SearchX holds no internal information, so it's safe to put in front of them.
04 RetailPass

Retail wanted a new in-store queue system, so we're building RetailPass to their vision, in close cooperation with them. It's simpler for the customer when everything sits in their mobile - ideally in OneApp.

Another one that, done the old way, would spend long months before we had anything to show. Today we put pieces in front of Retail as we go and tune it together.
05 Flow  (this one is BIG)

Flow is our new frontend - it replaces the Siebel UI, the Flex UI and a handful of other systems. And it does it so our agents (CC and retail) get one simple experience: all the complicated stuff is solved for them, instead of hopping through five systems entering and re-typing the same things by hand.

This one deserves its own issue, and it'll get one. For now, just know it's coming. Stay tuned.

Next time: a proper look inside Flow - what it takes out of our agents' hands, and what it means that we built it this fast. Until then, if any of these is close to something you're wrestling with, reply and let's talk.

Biggest credit goes to the team. I'm just the one writing it up.
