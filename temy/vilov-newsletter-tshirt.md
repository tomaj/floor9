## Surové poznámky / čísla

Take off the old t-shirt. Think new.

The power of AI in an engineer's hands - a story about how technology helps you break the boundaries of expertise, even when you think "I can't do this".

CONTEXT - An engineer who doesn't understand engines
Technical education? Yes. Combustion engines? No. Garden machinery and cars were never my domain.
Spring garden prep at my parents' place. A Szentkirály Kertgépek rotavator won't start after winter. Service shops are fully booked. Mom wants it done right now.

LESSON #1 - AI as an advisor, but not an infallible god
01 I asked AI - checklist: oil, spark plug, filters, carburetor.
02 I bought the kit - AI "confirmed" the engine name. It didn't fit.
Generation mismatch - AI couldn't see the label. I didn't verify it.
Takeaway: AI is a powerful helper, but humans must validate the results. Without critical thinking and verifying details, AI can lead you down a dead end.

LESSON #2 - Understanding the connections - Weekend expert
Precise inputs - I gave AI the exact data from the manufacturing label.
Step-by-step guidance - AI guided me through each step. Not just to fix it, but to understand it.
Result - The rotavator started. Garden done. New skill acquired.
Takeaway: With the right inputs, AI becomes the best teacher. For the first time, I understood how the machine works - not just "fix it", but truly comprehend it.

SYMBOLISM - Old t-shirt. New mindset.
During the repair, I was wearing an old, worn-out Telekom t-shirt that's 20 years old.
"I don't know how to do this." "This is not my job."
We cling to old identities and limits - just like we cling to old t-shirts that no longer hold together.

Main message: Take off the old t-shirt. With AI, nothing is impossible. The barriers between disciplines are fading.

For work: Every day we face tasks outside our expertise. AI lets us become experts in record time. The right tool in the hands of a brave person - don't be afraid of things that seem impossible.
